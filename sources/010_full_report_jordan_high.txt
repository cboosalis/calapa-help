Leadership Equity Inquiry Evidence Scaffold

Purpose of this draft:
Use this scaffold to begin a leadership equity inquiry evidence analysis. It organizes public outcome indicators into a preliminary, equity-centered area of educational need. The draft is not a final educational leadership inquiry response. Before using it, leadership teams and educational administration candidates should verify the public figures, add site-level evidence, consult relevant stakeholders, and narrow the focus to a specific student group, outcome concern, and local problem of practice.

School and context:
For 2024-25, Jordan High in Los Angeles Unified, Los Angeles, had 875 eligible students in the chronic absenteeism file. Of those students, 430 were chronically absent, for a schoolwide chronic absenteeism rate of 49.1%.

LEADERSHIP INQUIRY STARTING POINT

Initial student outcome evidence:
Leadership inquiry should begin with evidence of student outcomes, not with an assumed cause or preferred intervention. The numbered indicators below help leaders identify whether the strongest preliminary concern is attendance, ELA, Math, English learner progress and language access, suspension and discipline equity, graduation and completion, or a focused relationship among outcomes and local context.

Preliminary equity-centered focus:
Leadership teams should look for a defensible pattern in the public data: a schoolwide outcome concern, a subgroup outcome gap, a trend over time, or a comparison with district, county, or state reference points. The final equity inquiry focus should be narrower than this dashboard. It should name the student outcome, the student group or population, and the local evidence needed to understand the conditions contributing to the concern.

What this scaffold can and cannot support:
This scaffold can support an initial evidence-based rationale for selecting an area of educational need. It cannot, by itself, establish root cause, evaluate educator effectiveness, or prove that a local program or intervention caused the outcome pattern.

INTERPRETATION GUARDRAILS

These public data identify student outcome patterns, subgroup differences, English learner progress and language-access context, graduation/completion outcomes, and possible local context lenses. They should not be used by themselves to claim root cause, evaluate an intervention, or make claims about educator effectiveness.
