I created this site to help candidates, educators, and instructional leaders work more thoughtfully with public educational data.

This tool is intended to support preliminary evidence exploration. It helps users identify patterns in publicly available school, district, county, and state data, especially where subgroup trends may raise questions for further local inquiry.

ChatGPT supported the coding, debugging, technical development, and drafting of this resource. I provided the educational vision, reviewed the design and content, identified problems, and made the final decisions about what would be useful, responsible, and appropriate for leadership preparation.

This site is not a substitute for district records, local evidence, professional judgment, program guidance, or official California Department of Education documentation. Public aggregate data should be verified with local records, district personnel, and other official sources before being used in final leadership claims or action decisions.
